## Why these, and not photographs of shopping centres

The obvious search — "shopping mall" — returns photographs that are full of real
retail brands: shopfront signage, campaign posters, logos on every level. Those
were the first candidates and every one of them was rejected: a demo for an
invented holding cannot carry another company's trademarks, which is the same
reason the original photographs had to go.

So the selection is architecture without signage — facades, an atrium, a
concrete elevation. It also happens to sit better against the site's pale paper
ground than a brightly lit retail interior would.

generated by `building.py` in Blender. All original.
